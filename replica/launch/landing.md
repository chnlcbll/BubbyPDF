# Landing page (copy)

1. **Hero.** "Edit, fill and sign PDFs for free." Under it: "A Windows app that works offline. No account, no trial, nothing to cancel." Button: "Download for Windows". Image: `replica/screens-clone/02-editor.png` (re-shoot with a clean sample document first).
2. **The problem.** Basic PDF editing often sits behind a subscription, and free trials turn into annual plans with cancellation fees. Many readers also make you sign in and push your files to their cloud.
3. **How it works.** Download and open BubbyPDF. Drop in a PDF. Fill, sign or edit, then Save a copy.
4. **Features, fixes first.** Free for everything (fix 1). Offline, no sign-in (fix 2). Fill and sign with big, resizable check marks (fix 3). Edit existing text (fix 4). Undo, and Save writes a copy (fix 5). Merge, reorder, rotate all, extract (fix 6). Search with every match listed, and a Back button (fix 7). No pop-ups, no AI, dark mode (fix 8).
5. **Pricing.** "Free. That's the whole table."
6. **FAQ.** *Why is it free?* (answer honestly once you decide). *Does it upload my files?* No; it has no network access. *Why does Windows warn me?* The installer is not code-signed yet. *Can it edit any text?* It covers the old text and types new text on top; the original text is still in the file underneath. *Scanned PDFs?* No OCR yet.
7. **Final call.** "Download BubbyPDF" + "Windows 10 and 11, about 90 MB."
