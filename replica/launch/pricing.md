# Pricing

**BubbyPDF is free.** One tier, every feature, no account. There are no Stripe products to create.

## Why
- Pricing and paywalls are the top complaint about the original: 118 of 683 reviews, average 1.6 stars, all 3 sources.
- Trials, cancellation fees and refunds are second: 60 reviews, average 1.3 stars.
- Reviewers mention the prices they were charged (for example "$15 a month or $70 a year" in one Google Play review). These are reviewers' words, not an official price list.

## If you ever charge
Keep the editor free. Charge for something optional and separate (for example a signed, auto-updating build, or a business support plan). Never put existing free features behind a paywall: that is the exact move reviewers punished.
