## Logo brief
- Idea: a document sheet resting on a bench rail: a simple rectangle with a folded corner sitting on a horizontal bar.
- Type: symbol + wordmark; the symbol alone for the app icon.
- Must read at 16px (the fold and the bar only) and at 1024px.
- Deliverables: SVG, 256px .ico for Windows, 1024 PNG, 1200x630 social image.
- Must not resemble the original's mark: no red, no stylized "A", no triangular ribbon shapes.

## Voice: plain, calm, generous
- plain, not dull. calm, not passive. generous, not salesy.
- Do: "Save a copy". Don't: "Unlock Pro to export".
- Do: say what happened ("Saved to Downloads"). Don't: celebrate ("Awesome! 🎉").
- Do: explain errors and the fix. Don't: apologize vaguely.
- Do: assume the user owns their files. Don't: ask them to sign in.
- Do: one word labels on tools. Don't: invent feature brand names.
