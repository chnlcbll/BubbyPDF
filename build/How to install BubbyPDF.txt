BubbyPDF 1.3.2 - a free PDF editor for Windows
===============================================

Everything is free. No account, no trial, no ads, no internet needed.
Your files never leave your computer.

WHICH FILE DO I USE?
--------------------
  BubbyPDF-Setup-1.3.2.exe     Installs BubbyPDF (Start menu entry, "Open with" for PDFs).
  BubbyPDF-Portable-1.3.2.exe  No install: just double-click it to run.

You only need one of the two.

"WINDOWS PROTECTED YOUR PC"
---------------------------
The first time you run it, Windows may show a blue box saying
"Windows protected your PC". This happens because BubbyPDF is new and
not yet signed with a paid certificate. To continue:
  1. Click "More info".
  2. Click "Run anyway".
You only have to do this once.

WHAT YOU NEED
-------------
  Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit) and about 300 MB of free space.

WHAT IT DOES
------------
  Edit text and paragraphs, add text and images, highlight, underline,
  strike out, sticky notes, drawings, check marks, signatures (draw, type,
  or import a photo of yours), fill forms, redact, combine and split PDFs,
  reorder, rotate and delete pages, page numbers, watermarks, passwords,
  reduce file size, repair damaged PDFs, recognize text in scans (English),
  read aloud, print, and convert:
    PDF to Word, Excel, PowerPoint, images, HTML, Markdown and text;
    Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx, .csv), PowerPoint (.pptx), images,
    text and saved web pages to PDF.

OPTIONAL: EVEN BETTER OFFICE CONVERSIONS
----------------------------------------
BubbyPDF converts Word, Excel and PowerPoint files by itself. If LibreOffice
(free, libreoffice.org) is installed on the computer, BubbyPDF uses it
automatically for an exact copy of the layout, and for older formats such as
.doc, .xls, .ppt and .odt.
